All the Movies


Ripley holding a flamethrower and pulse rifle in the alien hive. (Aliens, 1986 - hollywoodrelics.com)

Here are the main Alien movies in the order they came out. Ellen Ripley is the main character in the first four.

Alien (1979)
Directed by Ridley Scott. The crew of the Nostromo brings an alien on board.
Aliens (1986)
Directed by James Cameron. Ripley goes back to LV-426 with the Marines.
Alien 3 (1992)
Directed by David Fincher. Ripley crash lands on a prison planet.
Alien: Resurrection (1997)
Directed by Jean-Pierre Jeunet. A clone of Ripley is made 200 years later.
Prometheus (2012)
Directed by Ridley Scott. A prequel about where humans came from.
Alien: Covenant (2017)
Directed by Ridley Scott. A colony ship finds a dangerous planet.
Alien: Romulus (2024)
Directed by Fede Alvarez. Young colonists find aliens on an old space station.

Spin-Offs and TV


A Predator and a Xenomorph playing chess instead of fighting. (Fan art, wallpaperflare.com)

These aren't part of the main movie list, but they are still part of the Alien world.

Alien vs. Predator (2004)
Directed by Paul W. S. Anderson. Explorers in Antarctica find an ancient pyramid where Predators hunt Xenomorphs as a test.
Aliens vs. Predator: Requiem (2007)
Directed by the Brothers Strause. A Predator and Xenomorph mix called the "Predalien" crash lands near a small town in Colorado.
Alien: Earth (2025)
A TV series created by Noah Hawley. It takes place in 2120, two years before the first movie, when a spaceship carrying alien creatures crashes on Earth.

I liked the main characters in Alien: Earth, and the pacing was good. What it lacked in big action scenes, it made up for with good suspense, great alien scenes, and shocking twists.

I think Alien vs. Predator should count. It has the Xenomorphs, and it also has the Predator, which I like too.

Did You Know?

Prometheus and Alien: Covenant came out later, but their stories happen before the first movie.

In the first movie, the crew finds a giant dead pilot called the "Space Jockey." Prometheus shows that it was really an Engineer wearing a suit.
