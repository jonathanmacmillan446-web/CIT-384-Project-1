All the Movies

Here are the main Alien movies in the order they came out. Ellen Ripley is the main character in the first four.

Alien (1979)
Directed by Ridley Scott. The crew of the Nostromo brings an alien on board.
Aliens (1986)
Directed by James Cameron. Ripley goes back to LV-426 with the Marines.
Alien 3 (1992)
Directed by David Fincher. Ripley crash lands on a prison planet.
Alien: Resurrection (1997)
Directed by Jean-Pierre Jeunet. A clone of Ripley is made 200 years later.
Prometheus (2012)
Directed by Ridley Scott. A prequel about where humans came from.
Alien: Covenant (2017)
Directed by Ridley Scott. A colony ship finds a dangerous planet.
Alien: Romulus (2024)
Directed by Fede Alvarez. Young colonists find aliens on an old space station.

Did You Know?

Prometheus and Alien: Covenant came out later, but their stories happen before the first movie.

In the first movie, the crew finds a giant dead pilot called the "Space Jockey." Prometheus shows that it was really an Engineer wearing a suit.
