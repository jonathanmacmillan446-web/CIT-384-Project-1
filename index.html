About This Site

This site is about the Alien franchise movies. The first movie came out in 1979 and was directed by Ridley Scott. It tells the story about the crew of a spaceship called the Nostromo who find a dangerous alien creature on a moon called LV-426.

Since then there have been seven main movies. I enjoy them because they mix science fiction with horror, and the xenomorph design is awesome as sci-fi creatures go.

What's on This Site

  • Colonial Marines - the soldiers from Aliens

Fun Fact

The alien's blood is acid. It can melt right through the floor of a spaceship.
